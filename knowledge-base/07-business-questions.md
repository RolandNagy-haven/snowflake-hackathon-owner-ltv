# 07 — Business Questions

Rachel Gregory opens the day (09:10) presenting these in person, focusing on lifetime value.
They come from the real requests. **Pick the ones your domain owns and build to answer them.**

## Rachel Gregory — Owner value and investment *(Owner LTV owns most of these)*

- What is an owner worth over their lifetime, **by park, pitch grade and van grade**?
- Which owners are **high-risk AND high-value**? (Today the churn programme treats all high-risk
  owners equally — it shouldn't.)
- What does an owner **cost to acquire, against what they're worth**?
- Which **acquisition profiles systematically produce short-tenure owners**?
- What's the **LTV impact of a pitch move, a part exchange, or an owner taking up lettings**?

## Performance Marketing — Spend allocation

- Which channels bring owners with the **highest lifetime value**, not just the most leads?
- What's the **LTV-to-CPA ratio by channel**?
- Where should **next quarter's budget** go?
- Which campaigns touch owners who go on to **stay five years or more**?

## Caravan Sales — Funnel efficiency

- Where in the funnel do prospects **drop out**, by park and by source?
- Which lead sources convert to a **completion**, not just to an appointment?
- How long does the journey from **first enquiry to completion** actually take?
- Which appointments **show**, and does the advisor make a difference?

## The integration question — needs all three domains (the demo)

> **"Show me acquisition channels ranked by the lifetime value of the owners they produced,
> against what we spent to get them."**

Performance Marketing's channel data + Top of Funnel's journey/cost + Owner LTV's value, joined
on the shared `HAVEN_ID` owner definition.

## Turning these into deliverables

- **12:30:** each team has a semantic view answering **one** real question from its list.
- **15:00:** draft **five verified queries** per team — pin a known question to a known method
  so the agent has no choice to make (this is the antidote to divergence — see
  [09](09-benchmark-and-divergence.md)). These are the highest-value output of the day.
